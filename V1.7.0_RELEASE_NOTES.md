# v1.7.0 — Quotations and Customer Receivables Final Audit

This release completes and hardens the quotation, POS payment, and customer-credit requirements.

## Quotation workflow

- Quotations are built and edited on a full page, without a creation popup.
- Every line supports a temporary quoted price plus percentage or fixed discount.
- Standard price, cost per unit, profit per unit, line profit, total cost, and estimated quotation profit are visible only to staff and are excluded from customer documents.
- Open quotations can be cancelled; expired quotations are detected automatically and can be reopened for review.
- Conversion now asks for payment method, amount paid/received, and an optional due date. It supports paid, partially paid, and credit invoices instead of silently marking every conversion paid.
- Conversion locks the quotation, revalidates active products/customer and current stock, and prevents duplicate invoices from concurrent clicks.

## Advanced customer and credit pipeline

- Customer types, credit limits, and default payment terms are configurable.
- Partial and unpaid sales receive a due date automatically.
- Customer profiles show every bill's goods, quantities, prices, payments, paid amount, due amount, due date, and overdue age.
- One account payment is allocated to the opening balance and then oldest unpaid invoices. Invoice payment rows and statuses are updated atomically, so customer, sales, dashboard, and receipt balances stay consistent.
- Accounts Receivable shows current versus overdue balances, open-bill count, oldest due date, and over-limit customers.
- The dashboard shows total and overdue receivables.
- Invoice and account receipts are linked to the cashier's open register, included in cash-in-hand, and counted on the day money is actually received.
- POS shows existing debt, debt added by the current cart, projected balance, payment terms, and a projected credit-limit warning.

## Accounting and validation fixes

- Cash tender above the invoice total records the invoice as paid only up to its total and keeps the remainder as change.
- Credit checkout without an entered deposit defaults to unpaid, not fully paid.
- Account and invoice overpayments are rejected; payment methods now support cash, card, bank transfer, cheque, and other recorded methods.
- Customer balances, credit limits, and payment terms are validated server-side.
- Additive startup migration creates the new due-date, payment-term, and payment-allocation columns and expands legacy payment-method enums without deleting data.

## Verification

Run from the project root:

```text
npm install
npm run verify
```

Existing databases are upgraded automatically on application startup. A backup before production rollout is still recommended.
